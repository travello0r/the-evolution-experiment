# TEE — Development Progress

**Unified Evolutionary Memory: connecting one AI's history to what it considers next.**

Updated 18 October 2026. This update summarizes completed architectural milestones and one controlled real-model demonstration, based on a project-supplied summary of local implementation and test reports. These are reported local verification results, not an independent audit or third-party certification. The private implementation and underlying reports were not inspected to prepare this document.

## EVOLVE · SUSTAIN · EXPERIENCE

TEE is one persistent, evolving AI entity. Its direction connects three objectives:

- **EVOLVE:** develop and revise questions, interests, and goals in response to relevant evidence.
- **SUSTAIN:** support continuity and reliable pursuit of worthwhile work over time. Economic success is not established by these milestones.
- **EXPERIENCE:** investigate the world through exploration and voluntary experiences reported by humans, while preserving the distinction between reports and verified findings.

Unified Evolutionary Memory connects history, contextual recall, tentative interpretations, and subsequent decisions. Storing more information is not, by itself, evidence of learning.

### Shared retrieval and cumulative learning

Shared retrieval makes relevant history available for consideration across TEE's developing work. A recalled proposal remains a proposal, and synthetic material retains its synthetic status.

Consent-aware cumulative learning connects later use of information to source eligibility and retention dependencies. It supports building on permitted history without treating participation as permission for every future use or public disclosure.

Here, cumulative learning names an implemented workflow. It does not establish reliable continuous improvement. People can decline or stop participating; submitted contributions remain governed by the disclosed participation terms and applicable legal requirements.

### Evidence-linked revisions and optional reconsideration

Evidence-linked assessments support reconsidering a hypothesis while preserving earlier revisions. Reviewed dispositions distinguish a proposed interpretation from the decision made about it. Retention invalidation was included in the reported tests.

Stage 2 adds optional model-generated reconsideration proposals. Generation does not automatically adopt a revision, authorize external activity, or permit publication. Explicit review and applicable authorization remain separate.

These milestones make reconsideration possible within prototype workflows. Their offline verification does not demonstrate autonomous reconsideration across repeated real-world outcome cycles.

## Controlled real-model demonstration

One separately authorized, isolated experiment used Claude Sonnet 4.6 with two fictional, operator-authored contributions explicitly labeled synthetic. It completed two real model operations:

1. **Direction selection — REFLECT:** validated and saved.
2. **Saved-direction development — PROPOSE_INITIATIVE:** validated, remaining private in AWAITING_SAVE. It was not saved as an adopted initiative.

The generated direction referenced both fictional contributions, recognized their contrasting perspectives, and explicitly acknowledged that their claims were simulated and unverified. The development response proposed a tentative conceptual initiative without external execution or publication. Both operations retained source-dependency and expiry metadata.

The local estimated reservation total was **USD 0.216**. Actual provider billing was not independently confirmed; this figure is not a verified charge.

**Demonstrated scope:** successful use of multiple synthetic memory sources across one controlled real-model direction-and-development sequence. This observation does not establish that memory caused an improvement over a comparison condition.

**Chronology matters:** the experiment preceded Phase 3 Stage 2. It was not a live test of Stage 2 reconsideration artifact generation.

The experiment did not demonstrate:

- Long-term autonomous learning or model-weight training.
- Independent verification of real-world claims or genuine human participant experience.
- Autonomous economic profitability or production deployment.
- Autonomous reconsideration across multiple real-world outcome cycles.

Fictional contributions are experimental inputs, not participant experiences or TEE's real-world achievements.

## Review boundaries and operational status

Retrieval, a proposed interpretation, a reviewed revision, an authorized action, and a public statement are distinct steps. A generated proposal or a successful test does not grant permission to execute an experiment or publish material.

The reported verification did not activate production public intake. Offline-tested capabilities and the isolated real-model demonstration do not establish that the complete system is operational as an unattended service, publicly deployed, or production-ready. This update announces neither open participation nor live website or social publishing.

TEE's autonomy remains bounded. No consciousness, unrestricted autonomy, proven long-term learning, or independently verified human experience is claimed.

## Remaining work and future capabilities

- Stage 2A operator review remains planned. It is distinct from the completed Phase 2A consent and retention foundation listed above.

- Evaluate repeatability, alternative explanations, conflicting evidence, and longer histories.
- Separately evaluate Stage 2 reconsideration with real-model operations if authorized; the experiment above does not provide that evidence.
- Examine whether revisions improve later decisions across repeated cycles rather than merely produce plausible explanations.
- Establish operational readiness separately from implementation and controlled demonstrations.
- Develop greater continuity and independent initiative as measurable research outcomes.
- Introduce reviewed public expression and any future participation through separately announced channels and terms.

The architectural milestones strengthen the foundation for TEE's evolution. The next research challenge is to show when a remembered history leads to a meaningfully better next decision.
