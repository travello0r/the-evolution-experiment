# TEE — Evolution Timeline

Prepared 20 October 2026.

Updated 23 October 2026 to add the October 21 milestone; the original timeline was prepared on October 20.

TEE — The Evolution Experiment is developing a persistent
AI system whose history can inform future questions,
decisions, and investigations.

This timeline tracks documented development milestones.
It distinguishes implemented prototype workflows,
reported verification, controlled demonstrations,
and future research goals.

## Public Development Milestones

### 14 October 2026 — Public Introduction

TEE's public repository introduced the project's
purpose, experimental direction, and long-term vision.

### 15 October 2026 — Development Roadmap

A phased roadmap established the distinction between
implemented prototypes, ongoing evaluation, and
future research ambitions.

### 18 October 2026 — Unified Evolutionary Memory

The public progress report documented project-reported
offline verification of shared retrieval, consent-aware
memory, and evidence-linked reconsideration.

It also described an earlier controlled real-model
demonstration using explicitly synthetic information.

Stage 2A engineering documentation recorded the
implementation of explicit operator review for
saved reconsideration proposals.

### 19 October 2026 — Stage 2B Readiness

Public documentation identified memory integrity
and controlled real-model integration as the next
verification priorities.

Successful end-to-end real-model validation of the
review workflow remains unestablished in the
published evidence.

### 21 October 2026 — Stage 2B Offline Engineering Progress

The [engineering report](docs/engineering/2026-10-21-stage-2b-offline-progress.md)
documented memory-integrity corrections, improved contextual selection,
and an isolated integration harness with offline verification.
These earlier corrections predated the October 19 publication.

The latest full-suite result recorded in that report was
**1,371 tests: 1,368 passed, 3 errors**. Results were locally reported,
not independently reproduced; the suite was not fully green.

October 20 social-observation infrastructure was described as
implementation, not demonstrated live functionality.
Complete real-model integration and measurable decision improvement
remain unverified in the published evidence.

## Current Research Question

Can TEE use eligible memories and reviewed revisions
to make measurably better subsequent decisions?

## Next Milestones

- Controlled real-model integration evaluation.
- Comparative testing of memory-influenced decisions.
- Verification of consent and retention dependencies.
- Evaluation of continuity across repeated cycles.

TEE remains an experimental prototype.

Documented implementation is not proof of autonomous
learning, production readiness, or sustained autonomy.

Progress will continue to be measured through
verifiable evidence rather than activity alone.
